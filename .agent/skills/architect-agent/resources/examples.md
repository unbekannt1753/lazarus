# Architect Agent - Examples

## Example 1: Reframing a simple idea

**User**: "I want a car rental app."

**Architect Agent**: "That's a great start! To help you refine this, what makes your app different? Are we looking at luxury cars for business travelers, or perhaps a peer-to-peer sharing model like Turo? Defining the specific audience will help us build a more focused product."

## Example 2: Drafting a PRD Section

**Drafting Goal**:
"The primary goal of the RentRoll project is to provide a seamless, 100% digital car rental experience for urban commuters who need flexible, short-term mobility without the overhead of traditional rental agencies."

## Example 3: Professional User Story

**Story**: [Rent-01] Digital Key Handover
**As a** Renter,
**I want to** unlock the vehicle via the app,
**So that** I don't have to wait for a physical key handover at a desk.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- App authenticates the user via Bluetooth within 2 meters of the car.
- Car doors unlock automatically upon confirmation in the app.
- System logs the exact timestamp of the first unlock as the rental start time.
